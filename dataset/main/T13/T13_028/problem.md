A food-processing group operates 20,000 workshops, listed in `workshops` in `instance.json`. Every
workshop is assigned to exactly one of its 10 listed production recipes; assigning a workshop to a
recipe means running that recipe for the whole of the workshop's daily campaign.

Each recipe has four numbers: it draws `steam` units of process steam from the shared boiler named by
`boiler` (an integer index into `boiler_capacity`), it produces `output` units of saleable product, and
it emits `carbon` units of CO2. The group has committed to a per-workshop CO2 ceiling, given in
`workshop_carbon_budget` at the same index as the workshop.

Steam is generated by 6 shared boilers, and each boiler can supply at most the value in
`boiler_capacity` (same index as the `boiler` field) units of steam per day across all workshops.

Decide the production recipe mix of every workshop so that no boiler quota is exceeded, no workshop
CO2 ceiling is exceeded, every workshop's campaign is fully allocated across its recipes, and the total
`output` produced by the group is as large as possible.

Report the maximum total output and the recipe mix of every workshop.

All numerical data are fixed and provided in the instance file.

## Data schema

The complete fixed instance is in the instance file; no values are generated or sampled during execution.

- `workshops`: array with 20,000 records.
  Record fields:
  1. `recipes` (array with 10 records)
     Record fields:
     1. `boiler` (integer): index of the shared boiler this recipe draws steam from.
     2. `steam` (integer): steam units drawn from that boiler when the recipe is used.
     3. `output` (integer): saleable product units produced when the recipe is used.
     4. `carbon` (integer): CO2 units emitted when the recipe is used.
- `boiler_capacity`: array with 6 integers; per-boiler daily steam quota.
- `workshop_carbon_budget`: array with 20,000 integers; per-workshop CO2 ceiling.

A workshop's recipe assignment may be split across its recipes; the split fractions are nonnegative and
sum to one for each workshop.
